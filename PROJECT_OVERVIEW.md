## 1. The Big Picture: What is TrueShift?

Imagine the traditional way people mark attendance at an office, school, or factory:
* Swiping plastic ID cards,
* Pressing dirty fingerprint scanners that often misread,
* Or signing names in paper registers and Excel spreadsheets.

**TrueShift replaces all of that with a smart, easy-to-use digital hub.**

With TrueShift:
* **No expensive hardware is needed:** Any laptop, tablet, or phone camera with a web browser becomes a secure check-in terminal.
* **Attendance is lightning-fast and honest:** Employees stand in front of the camera for 2 seconds. The system confirms who they are, verifies that they are a real living human (not a printed photo or phone screen), and records their exact check-in time.
* **HR work becomes effortless:** Vacation requests, shift schedules, overtime, and late arrivals are handled automatically.
* **Proactive management:** Instead of just reporting what happened last month, TrueShift acts like a weather forecast—warning managers in advance if someone is showing signs of chronic absence or burnout.

### Problem 1: Time Theft & "Buddy Punching"
* **The Reality:** When companies use plastic access cards, PINs, or QR codes, employees easily lend them to coworkers who swipe them in when they are actually running late or staying home.
* **The Financial Cost:** Industry studies estimate that companies lose up to **16% of their payroll costs** paying for unworked hours and fraudulent attendance.
* **The Security Flaw:** Basic photo recognition can often be tricked by holding up a colleague's photo from social media or a selfie video on a phone screen.

### Problem 2: Administrative Friction & Spreadsheet Nightmares
* **The Reality:** In many organizations, asking for a day off involves back-and-forth emails, paper forms, or messy WhatsApp messages.
* **Human Errors:** HR managers often end up manually entering numbers into Excel at the end of every month. Balances get miscalculated, approvals get lost, and arguments arise over whether someone arrived within the "10-minute grace period" or not.
* **Wasted Productivity:** Highly paid HR and operations professionals spend upwards of **40% of their weekly time** sorting through routine paperwork rather than hiring, coaching, and supporting employees.

### Problem 3: The "Rearview Mirror" Trap (Zero Predictive Foresight)
* **The Reality:** Standard software only records the past: *"Employee X was absent on Tuesday."*
* **The Consequence:** Management is always reacting too late. When key employees experience quiet burnout, start showing irregular schedules, or begin missing days repeatedly, the company doesn't discover the underlying problem until a critical project misses a deadline or the employee resigns.

## 4. How It Works in Daily Life (Step-by-Step)

Here is how simple TrueShift is for everyday users:

### Step 1: Getting Set Up (One-Time Onboarding)
1. An administrator creates an employee profile (Department: *Design*, Shift: *9:00 AM – 5:00 PM*).
2. The employee sits in front of the camera for 30 seconds to capture three angles of their face (center, slight left, slight right).
3. The system converts their facial traits into a secure digital code (not an image file) and stores it safely.

### Step 2: Everyday Check-In (Takes ~2 seconds)
1. The employee arrives at the office or opens their work laptop.
2. They look at the camera.
3. TrueShift checks:
   * **Is it really them?** (Match confirmed in less than a second).
   * **Are they live?** (Checks for natural blinking or slight head movement).
   * **Are they within their shift window?** (Records them as *On Time* or *Late* based on company policy).
4. A green confirmation badge pops up: **"Welcome, Alex! Checked in at 8:56 AM."**
5. If someone tries to check in twice on the same day, the system politely notifies them that today's check-in has already been registered.

### Step 3: Taking Time Off (Leave & Holidays)
1. Need a vacation or sick day? The employee opens the **Leave** tab, picks the dates, and clicks *Apply*.
2. Their manager receives a clean dashboard notification with one-click **Approve** or **Reject**.
3. Once approved, the employee's available leave balance is instantly updated, and payroll reports reflect the change with zero manual math.

### Step 4: Behind the Scenes (Smart Early Warnings)
1. TrueShift analyzes broad attendance patterns over weeks.
2. If an employee's attendance rhythm suddenly shifts or shows signs of repetitive absenteeism, the HR dashboard displays an alert:
   * *“Attention: Alex’s attendance pattern indicates high absence risk over the upcoming cycle.”*
3. HR can proactively reach out to Alex to ask if everything is okay or offer flexibility, preserving employee well-being and project continuity.

## 7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### Q1: Does the company need to buy specialized biometric cameras or fingerprint readers?
> **No.** TrueShift runs entirely inside modern web browsers (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Safari, Firefox). You can use the built-in webcam on a standard office laptop, a wall-mounted tablet, or an existing kiosk.

#### Q2: Can an employee trick the camera using a high-resolution photo or mobile video of a coworker?
> **No.** TrueShift has built-in **liveness detection**. It verifies optical depth and natural biological markers (like eye blinks and subtle head movement). A static photograph or screen playback will fail this test.

#### Q3: Does the system store raw photos of employees' faces?
> **No.** Privacy and data security come first. The system transforms facial landmarks into encrypted mathematical numbers (vectors). The actual photos are not stored or sold, meaning identity data remains secure.

#### Q4: What happens if an employee arrives a few minutes late?
> TrueShift allows companies to configure customized **grace periods** (e.g., 10 minutes). If an employee arrives within that window, they are marked present. If they exceed it, the system marks them as late, ensuring consistent and fair policy enforcement.

#### Q5: Can someone accidentally or intentionally check in multiple times in one day?
> **No.** TrueShift features built-in duplicate check-in guards. Once an employee is checked in for their designated shift, repeated attempts on that day are locked out.
